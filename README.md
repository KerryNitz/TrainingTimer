# TrainingTimer

TrainingTimer provides timers for structured exercise routines:
- EMOM10: an **E**very **M**inute **O**n the **M**inute timer for 10 minutes, with a lead in timer of 15 seconds and beeps 3 seconds before each minute
- EMOM20: an **E**very **M**inute **O**n the **M**inute timer for 20 minutes, with a lead in timer of 15 seconds and beeps 3 seconds before each minute
- TUT30/30x10: a **T**ime **U**nder **T**ension timer with 30 seconds on, 30 seconds off for 10 sets, with a lead in timer of 15 seconds and beeps 3 seconds before each interval
- TUT60/60x10: a **T**ime **U**nder **T**ension timer with 60 seconds on, 60 seconds off for 10 sets, with a lead in timer of 15 seconds and beeps 3 seconds before each interval
- TUT90/30x10: a **T**ime **U**nder **T**ension timer with 90 seconds on, 30 seconds off for a configurable number of sets, with a lead in timer of 15 seconds and beeps 3 seconds before each interval
- CustomTUT: a **T**ime **U**nder **T**ension timer with configurable seconds on, seconds off, number of rounds, and number of exercises, with a lead in timer of 15 seconds and beeps 3 seconds before each interval (number of rounds x number of exercises = number of sets)

These timers are especially suitable for kettlebell and heavy club routines.
